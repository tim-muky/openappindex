# The questionnaire as fielded

### The cost of being found: every question, answer option and skip rule

This page is generated from study/survey.py, the same definition that built the survey form, so it shows exactly what respondents see. Question IDs (S1, D2, M4…) are the ones the [study protocol](https://openappindex.org/study/protocol.html) refers to. Survey: [tally.so/r/obr9AP](https://tally.so/r/obr9AP?channel=study-page).

## Page 1

*Carried by the invitation link, not shown: token, channel, group.*

A short survey of app publishers, above all new and small ones. About 12 minutes.

This survey asks app publishers how people actually find their apps, and what it costs to be found. There are no right answers, and "don't know" is a useful one: how much publishers know about their own install sources is part of what we want to learn.

**What happens with your answers**

• Results are published in aggregate in an open research paper (spring 2027). The aggregated results are also released as open data under the Creative Commons Attribution licence (CC BY 4.0): anyone may reuse them, as long as they name the source. Individual answers are never released.

• Your answers are anonymous unless you choose otherwise. The survey does not ask for your email address, and a name is asked only if you want to be quoted by name. At the end you can allow us to compare your answers with the public data we measured for your app; only then do we ask for its App Store link. Quotes from your free-text answer are used only if you explicitly allow it.

• Raw answers are seen only by the study lead and are deleted 12 months after the paper is published. The answers are analysed with the help of an AI service (Claude, by Anthropic), which receives them only after the name and App Store link fields have been removed. Please don't write personal details into free-text answers. Details are in the privacy notice.

• Nothing is for sale and nothing is asked of you beyond the answers. Taking part changes nothing about how your app appears in openAPPindex.

• You can skip any question. "Don't know" is always a valid answer.

• You can withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (GDPR Art. 7(3)), by emailing tim@study.openappindex.org. Because answers are anonymous, we can find yours only if you tell us your App Store link or roughly when you answered.

Everyone who takes part and leaves an email address at the end receives the paper before it is published, and the results for their app category.

**Who runs this**

Tim Meyerdierks, openAPPindex (openappindex.org), Berlin. Before this, ten years as COO of a mid-sized marketing company in Berlin, Bonial.com (kaufDA in Germany), spending millions of euros on app installs every year. He also publishes small apps himself (e.g. galleybook.com); they are excluded from the study. The study protocol was fixed and published before the survey opened: https://openappindex.org/study/protocol.html
