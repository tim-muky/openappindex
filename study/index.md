# The cost of being found

### A study of how apps get found, and what it costs their publishers

**Open from 22 October 2026 to 31 January 2027 · about 12 minutes · results published openly in spring 2027**

**[Take the survey →](https://tally.so/r/obr9AP?channel=study-page)**

---

## What the study asks

openAPPindex has measured two categories of the German App Store, cooking and sleep, from public
data. This study asks the people behind those apps, the app publishers: **how do your users
actually find your app, and what does it cost you to be found?**

It is aimed above all at new and small publishers, though everyone who has published an iOS app
is welcome, including publishers of apps that are no longer available.

## How it works

- **A short survey, about 12 minutes**, with ranges rather than exact figures, and "don't know"
  always allowed.
- **The survey doesn't ask who you are.** A name is asked only if you want to be quoted by name.
  Your App Store link is optional; comparing your answers with our measurements needs a separate
  consent.
- **Nothing is for sale, and nothing is asked of you beyond your answers.** Taking part changes
  nothing about how your app appears in openAPPindex.
- **Results are open:** an open research paper, with the aggregated data under CC BY 4.0.
  Participants who want it receive the paper before publication.
- **A snapshot of your app:** participants can also get a one-page snapshot of how their app
  shows up in our measurements: its position for the most obvious search, how many of 25 common
  searches find it, and its rank among similar apps. It is made from public App Store data only,
  never from survey answers. [See an example](https://openappindex.org/study/snapshot-example.html)
  (a fictional app).
